Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 10, 2026

You may request deletion of an Omnicus account, workspace data, or data received through a connected Meta/WhatsApp Business account. There is no fee for making a request.

Request deletion

  1. Ask an authorized Omnicus workspace administrator to remove your user account or the affected project, if that option is available to you.
  2. Otherwise, email privacy@omnicus.app from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject Data deletion request.
  3. State whether the request concerns your user account, a named Omnicus workspace/project, a WhatsApp Business Account or phone number, or specific customer/contact data. Include only the identifiers needed to locate it. Do not send passwords, access tokens or message contents.
  4. We will verify that you are the data subject or are authorized to act for the relevant organization. We may ask for limited additional information when necessary to prevent an unauthorized deletion.

We acknowledge verified requests and aim to complete them within 30 days. If applicable law allows or requires more time, we will explain the extension and expected completion date.

Disconnect Meta and WhatsApp

An authorized project administrator can stop future WhatsApp processing by opening the project's Channels page in Omnicus and disconnecting the WhatsApp channel. An authorized Meta Business administrator can also remove Omnicus from the business's Meta integrations. Removing authorization stops future access but does not by itself delete records the business previously instructed Omnicus to store; request deletion using the steps above.

What happens after a verified request

Depending on the request and the requester's authority, we delete or irreversibly de-identify the relevant account, credentials, contact data, message content, media and connected-channel records from active systems. We also disable associated sessions and future channel access. If Omnicus processes the data for a customer organization, we may route the request to that organization or require its instructions before deleting its business records.

Limited retention

Some minimal records may be retained when required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or proof that a request was completed. Deleted information may remain in protected backups until the normal backup cycle expires and is not restored to active use. Raw valid channel webhooks and technical logs are normally removed within 30 days, and protected audit records are normally removed or archived within 180 days, unless a longer period is legally required.

Questions

For more information, read the Privacy Policy or email privacy@omnicus.app.
