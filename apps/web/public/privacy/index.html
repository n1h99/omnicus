Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Omnicus ("Omnicus", "we", "us" or "our") processes personal data when organizations and their authorized users use the Omnicus customer messaging and automation service (the "Service").

1. Our role

For account administration, security and our direct relationship with an organization, Omnicus determines why and how the relevant personal data is processed. When an organization uses the Service to communicate with its own customers or contacts, that organization is the data controller and Omnicus processes the data on its documented instructions as a service provider or processor. Requests about a business's customer records should normally be sent to that business first.

2. Data we process

  • Account and workspace data: name, business email, role, permissions, project membership and authentication records.
  • Customer and communication data: names, phone numbers, email addresses, tags, custom fields, consent or suppression status, messages, attachments, templates and delivery events provided by a customer or a connected channel.
  • WhatsApp Business data: WhatsApp Business Account and business phone identifiers, authorization grants, message and template data, webhook events and delivery status. Business access tokens are encrypted and are not displayed again after connection.
  • Integration data: identifiers and records exchanged with integrations configured by the organization, including Telegram, CRM and email delivery services.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, user agent, timestamps, correlation identifiers, sign-in and audit events, diagnostics and service health data.

3. How we obtain data

We receive data from authorized users, their organization, people who communicate with that organization, connected providers such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Telegram, configured CRM or email services, and automatically from use of the Service.

4. Why we process data

  • to provide accounts, workspaces, messaging, automation, broadcasts and support;
  • to connect and operate channels requested by an authorized organization;
  • to deliver messages, receive replies and display delivery or engagement status;
  • to secure the Service, prevent abuse, investigate incidents and maintain audit records;
  • to monitor reliability and improve product performance; and
  • to comply with law and enforce our agreements and acceptable-use requirements.

Depending on the context and applicable law, processing is based on performing a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing a business service, compliance with legal obligations, or consent obtained by the responsible organization.

5. WhatsApp and Meta data

Omnicus uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform APIs only to provide functionality selected by the organization: connecting a WhatsApp Business Account and phone number, subscribing the app to account webhooks, synchronizing message templates, sending and receiving business messages, and reporting message status. We do not sell Meta or WhatsApp data, use it for unrelated advertising, or share it except as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, or follow the organization's instructions.

6. Service providers and disclosures

Data may be disclosed only as needed to:

  • operate connected services selected by the customer, such as Meta/WhatsApp, Telegram, Resend and the customer's configured CRM;
  • use contracted hosting, storage, security and operational providers;
  • comply with a valid legal request or protect users, the public and the Service; or
  • complete a corporate transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection measures.

We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention

Business records are retained while the customer maintains the relevant workspace or as instructed by that customer, subject to contractual and legal requirements. Raw valid channel webhook payloads and technical logs are normally retained for up to 30 days. Audit and protected operational records are normally retained for up to 180 days. Media follows the workspace's configured retention period and may be retained while an active automation or template still references it. Backups and records required for fraud prevention, disputes or law may remain for a limited additional period before deletion or irreversible isolation.

8. Security

We use role-based access controls, encryption of channel credentials, restricted production access, request validation, audit records and other organizational and technical safeguards. No service can guarantee absolute security, and customers must protect their own accounts, devices and administrator access.

9. International processing

Providers and connected services may process data in countries other than the user's. Where required, the responsible party uses recognized contractual or legal safeguards for those transfers.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, or withdraw consent. Authorized Omnicus users should first contact their workspace administrator. A person whose data belongs to one of our customers should contact that business; we assist it with verified requests. You may also follow our data deletion instructions.

11. Children

The Service is a business product and is not directed to children. Organizations must not use it to collect children's data without every authorization and safeguard required by law.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Service or law changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Privacy and data requests can be sent to privacy@omnicus.app. Please do not email passwords, access tokens or message contents.
